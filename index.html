About

Why both BME and mechanical engineering?

I’m interested in the point where a clinical need becomes a physical system: a mechanism, sensor, enclosure, test plan, or manufacturing decision. Studying both fields gives me room to understand the medical context while building a deeper mechanical foundation.

At Duke, my work has included medical-device teams, global health design, embedded electronics, CAD, and physical testing. I’m still early in my engineering education, and this portfolio will keep changing as the projects do.

What I work with

Design and prototyping

Fusion 360, Onshape, mechanical drawings, concept screening, physical prototyping

Electronics and programming

Arduino, Java, Python, circuit assembly, soldering, sensor and relay integration

Testing and communication

Sensor calibration, thermal testing, data analysis, technical posters, presentations, LaTeX
